About ShARE

ShARE is a start-up at the crossroads of education and consulting, with “Do Well, Do Good” as its mission.

It aims to transform the way corporate organisations conventionally work, and to create a new generation of leaders and changemakers who do well for themselves and do good for the whole world.

The Future Leaders Programme is a leadership programme for university students worldwide. It turns bright students into Do Well Do Good leaders, and helps corporates serve both those leaders and the society around them.

“Do Well Do Good, the only way to make a change today.”

Tony Blair, Global Mentor of ShARE and former Prime Minister of the United Kingdom

At BITS Pilani
We are students who take up projects to deepen our knowledge of Consulting, VC, Product, Technology and Growth. Members get training, live projects, internship opportunities, resume-building sessions, real-world experience and exposure to a global community.
